Our Story

Built by Students. Built for Stories.

Why We Started

Parava Publications began as a student initiative to solve one problem: talented voices remain unheard due to lack of access.

Traditional publishing is often inaccessible, expensive, or gatekept. We chose to build a transparent alternative — one that prioritizes young writers and creative independence.

Our Values

Authors retain their rights. Your story belongs to you.

Transparency

No hidden fees. No hidden clauses.

Quality

Professional editing, formatting, and cover design standards.
