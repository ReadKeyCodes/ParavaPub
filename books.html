Crystal Wings Vol. 1

Arakiri Sen'on


Karma only wanted to lead a normal high school life until he stepped into World Technical High.
Amid student council turmoil, with a first-year plotting to seize power, Karma also faces his own personal struggles—cryptic flashbacks about the ‘Ruler’s Progeny’ and fragmented memories of his orphanage life. But most importantly…
His cyborg transformation.
Amid all the chaos, a shady organization transformed him into a cyborg. This dragged him into the cyborg underworld, forcing him to make enemies to save his friend, Asuna.
Now, Karma must live a dual life, keeping the secret of his cyborg identity. Will he succeed? Or will his secrets be exposed?
