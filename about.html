Our Story

Built by Students. Built for Stories.

Why We Started

Parava Publications was founded by students who experienced firsthand how difficult it is to be taken seriously as young writers.

We saw talented classmates abandon manuscripts because they believed they were "too young," "not connected enough," or "not ready." We saw creativity shrink under the weight of gatekeeping and silence.

Traditional publishing often feels distant and inaccessible — especially for students navigating academics, finances, and self-doubt at the same time.

So we decided to build something we wish we had. A platform that values ambition over age. Effort over connections. Voice over status.

Parava exists to ensure that being a student is not a limitation — it is a beginning.



Who We Are

We are a student-led publishing initiative based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. What began as a small idea among peers has grown into a structured platform supporting emerging authors.

We understand the hesitation that comes with sharing your writing. We understand the fear of rejection. And we understand the courage it takes to submit your work.

That is why our process is built on clarity, mentorship, and respect. Whether you choose full independence or a selective partnership, our goal remains the same: to help you publish with dignity and confidence.
